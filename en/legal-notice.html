About the content

“Waffensachkunde” is an independent study app for Android. The questions and answers are taken from the question catalogue for the expertise examination under § 7 WaffG published by the Bundesverwaltungsamt (Federal Office of Administration), dated 16 December 2024. This app is not a product of the Bundesverwaltungsamt. English translations and explanations are supplementary learning content.

2. Summary

The “Waffensachkunde” app works without a user account and without advertising. Your learning progress, answers, bookmarks and exam history are stored only on your device and are not sent to us or to third parties. Optionally, and switchable off at any time, the app sends anonymous usage statistics.

3. Data stored locally

Your device stores learning progress, bookmarks, exam results and app settings (e.g. language). This data does not leave your device. It may be included in iOS device backups (e.g. iCloud Backup), depending on your iOS settings. In Settings you can delete your progress.

4. Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

If you leave “Anonymous usage statistics” switched on, the app sends anonymous events to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck UG, Germany, EU servers), e.g. that a study session was started or abandoned, a practice exam was finished, or the purchase screen was shown. No question content, no answers, no individual progress, and no names or email addresses are sent. TelemetryDeck additionally receives technical context such as app version, iOS version, device model, language and region. A user identifier is derived from a random identifier and hashed again on the server with a salt, so it cannot be traced back to you. There is no tracking across other apps or websites.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG) where the statistics require access to your device, and otherwise our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can switch statistics off at any time under Settings → Statistics; no events are sent afterwards. More information: telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

5. Purchase of the full version

The full version is a one-time purchase (no subscription) processed through the Apple App Store. Only Apple processes payment data; we receive no payment or account data. The app checks whether the full version is unlocked directly through StoreKit on your device. Apple's privacy notices apply.

6. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is transmitted.

7. External links

The app links to external sources (e.g. Federal Office of Administration, gesetze-im-internet.de). They open in the browser or a web view, where the respective provider's privacy notices apply. The app's legal texts are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.), which may technically process your IP address.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Because we store no personal data about you that we could attribute to you, access and erasure requests are largely moot; for questions, contact the email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children

The app is intended for people preparing for the expertise examination under § 7 of the German Weapons Act (WaffG), which has minimum-age requirements.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is available here and in the app.

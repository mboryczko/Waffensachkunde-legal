2. Summary

The “Waffensachkunde” Android app works without a user account and without advertising. Your learning progress, answers, bookmarks and exam results are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us. The app sends pseudonymous usage statistics without question content, answers or learning progress. They are on by default and can be switched off at any time.

3. Data stored locally

Your device stores: answers and learning progress, bookmarks, exam results and an interrupted exam, your settings (e.g. language, theme, examining body and exam format, exam date, reminder) and whether the full version is unlocked. We do not process this data; the app does not send it off the device.

If you have enabled backup to your Google Account in the Android settings, Android may include this data in the backup. The backup is performed by Google, and Google's privacy notices apply. Under Settings → “Reset progress” you delete answers, bookmarks and exam results. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

4. Usage statistics (on by default, can be switched off)

The app sends usage events to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck UG, Germany, EU servers), which processes them on our behalf (data processing under Art. 28 GDPR). Recorded are the start of an app session and usage events, such as starting or abandoning a study session or practice exam, the chosen examining body, the exam format and opening the purchase screen. In addition, technical information such as the app and Android versions, device model and language, and a hashed identifier of the app installation are sent; TelemetryDeck hashes it again before storing it.

No question content, answers, learning progress, exam results, names or email addresses are sent. The data is pseudonymous: we cannot attribute it to a person. There is no tracking across other apps or websites.

The purpose is to improve the app: to see which features are used, where study sessions or exams are abandoned and which exam formats are in demand. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an app that works well and meets users' needs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); for the installation identifier stored on your device for this purpose we rely on § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG. TelemetryDeck only evaluates the data in aggregate.

Right to object: You can object to the statistics at any time (Art. 21 GDPR) by switching them off during app setup or under Settings → Privacy → “Usage statistics (TelemetryDeck)”. No further events are sent afterwards, and events not yet sent are discarded. More information: telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

5. Purchase of the full version

The full version is a one-time purchase (no subscription) through Google Play. Google handles payment and the purchase; we receive no payment or account data. Through the Google Play Billing Library, the app checks whether your Google Account holds a purchase and acknowledges it to Google Play. Google's privacy notices apply.

6. Rating the app

After a successful study round, the app may show the Google Play rating dialog. Whether and how you rate is processed by Google only; the app does not tell us.

7. Reminders and widget

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device; Android asks for the notification permission for this. The home-screen widget shows your progress from the local data. No data is transmitted.

8. External links

The app links to external sources (e.g. Bundesverwaltungsamt, gesetze-im-internet.de). They open in the browser, where the respective provider's privacy notices apply. These legal texts are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.), which may technically process your IP address.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR; for usage statistics see section 4). Because we store no data that we could attribute to you, we usually cannot match access or erasure requests to any record; for questions, contact the email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

10. Audience

The app is intended for people preparing for the expertise examination under § 7 of the German Weapons Act (WaffG), which sets a minimum age.

11. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is available here and in the app.
